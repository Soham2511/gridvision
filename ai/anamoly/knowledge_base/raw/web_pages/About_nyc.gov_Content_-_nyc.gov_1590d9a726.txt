About nyc.gov content
nyc.gov is the official website of New York City government. The content on nyc.gov is made for New Yorkers by government employees or officials.
We aim to be the most accurate and up-to-date online source for anything related to NYC government.
If you notice a mistake, please let us know so we can fix it.

Artificial intelligence and nyc.gov content
Content on nyc.gov is usually written by city employees or officials.
Sometimes, we use artificial intelligence (AI) tools to help with website content.
AI tools help us:
Fix writing mistakes
Make content easier to understand
Translate content into different languages
We don’t typically use AI tools to generate content. If we do, we’ll make it clear which content was AI-generated.
It’s also our policy not to use any AI-generated imagery on nyc.gov.
