Forum Information
Department of Public Service Staff (Staff) will host a stakeholder forum on
Wednesday, November 4th, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 3:15 p.m
., held in the 4th floor board room of the Department’s New York City office located at 90 Church St, New York, NY 10007 with a virtual option via Webex.
This meeting continues the series of Low- and Moderate-Income Energy Efficiency and Building Electrification (LMI EE/BE) Quarterly Forums that Staff are convening to update stakeholders on the performance of programs in the LMI EE/BE portfolio and facilitate discussion of challenges, opportunities, and emerging issues.
Stakeholders should note that present date of the forum was changed from the tentative date shared at previous forums in order to better incorporate forthcoming LMI EE/BE Implementation Plan updates.

Information for In-Person Attendees
Please check in at the security desk to enter the building. Stakeholders who register to attend in person by the 10/30 deadline will be added to the security list. You may be required to show photo identification. The conference will begin promptly at 10:00 a.m., so please arrive early to check in. Lunch will not be provided but attendees are welcome to bring a bagged lunch into the meeting room, which will remain open to attendees through the lunch break. Attendees will need to check-out and check-in with security during lunch if leaving the premises.
