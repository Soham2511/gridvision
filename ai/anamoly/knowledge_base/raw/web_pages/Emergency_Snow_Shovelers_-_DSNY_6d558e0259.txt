Emergency Snow Shovelers
Get paid to help keep New Yorkers safe after winter storms.
Emergency Snow Shovelers are called on after heavy snowfalls to remove snow and ice citywide from public areas like bus stops, crosswalks, fire hydrants, and step streets.
We recruit temporary, per diem (by day) shovelers as needed.
Pay starts at
$30.00
per hour and increases to
$45.00
per hour
after the first 40 hours worked in a week
.
**REGISTRATION PERK**
If your registration is completed before
November 30, 2026
, you will be eligible for a
PAID 4-hour orientation
in December, as well as fast-tracked processing to receive a paycheck within
2 weeks
of working.
To become an Emergency Snow Shoveler, attend an upcoming
Community Registration Event
or visit the
Jobs NYC Center
in Manhattan.
Learn more about Snow Shoveler Registration Events.
Eligibility
To be eligible for hire, you must meet the following criteria:
Age:
At least 18 years old
Work Status:
Legal authorization to work in the United States
Physical Ability:
Able to perform heavy, physically demanding labor in adverse, winter weather conditions.
Registration
You have two convenient ways to register as an Emergency Snow Shoveler:
you can either attend a
community registration event
or visit the dedicated
Jobs NYC Center
.
Here is how the two options break down:

Signing up for a Community Registration Event guarantees you an appointment time, but walk-ins are also welcome and no qualified applicant will be turned away. Please check back for new dates and pop-up locations.

What to Bring to Register
To ensure you can complete your registration on-site, you must bring the following items to your community event or to the
Jobs NYC Center
:
Required
Contact Info:
A valid phone number and email address
Identification:
Your Social Security card (original only - no photo/copy; this will enable timely payment)
Employment Eligibility:
Original and unexpired documents from the
official I-9 Acceptable Documents list
Optional
To speed up your registration, you
may
also bring the following completed forms for tax purposes:
W-4 (Federal Withholding)
IT-2014 (NYS Withholding)
What Happens Next
Email Confirmation:
You will receive an official confirmation via email of your registration 3 weeks following the registration event. Didn’t receive a confirmation email? Contact
ShovelSnow@dsny.nyc.gov
after October 31 to check the status of your registration.
Paid Orientation:
You will receive an invitation to attend a 4-hour fully-paid orientation.
Work Location:
You will be assigned a
DSNY garage
to report to when you are scheduled to work an emergency snow shoveler shift. Locations will be assigned based on borough needs and the top 3 preferences you submit during the registration process.
On-the-Job Requirements
You will be notified about shift assignments by email/phone. When you are called in to work a shift,
you MUST:
Bring proof of advanced registration
by presenting your confirmation email (either show printed copy or on your device) to your assigned work location.
Be physically fit
and fully capable of heavy, manual snow shoveling.
Wear your own proper, weather-resistant clothing
suitable for extreme winter conditions.
