Why become a Registered User of DMM?
Manage Subscriptions
New Cases – By subscribing to New Cases, an email notification will be sent to your email address when new cases that meet your criteria are started by the Commission.
Service Lists –By subscribing to a service list for a case, an email notification will be sent to you when a filing and/or issuance is entered in the case. Subscribers can request to receive “Commission Issuances Only” or “Filings and Commissions Issuances”.
Party List - Generally, parties commit to contribute to the development of a complete record in a proceeding by conducting discovery, submitting testimony, briefs, or other formal written comments, and/or participating in evidentiary hearings, procedural conferences and other formal events conducted in the case.
Manage Favorite Searches
Set up and save your favorite search criteria. Once saved, you can re-use the search so that you can quickly and easily find cases or documents in our files.
Comment on Cases
Conveniently post your opinions regarding cases before the Commission. Visit
How to Post Comments and Receive Updates on PSC Cases
for more information.
Update Contact Information
Update your contact information such as address, email, etc.
E-File
To be authorized for E-Filing, users are asked to complete and submit a Contact Form to NYS DPS. The form and instructions can be found at our
Electronic Filing Registration Instructions
page. After receipt and review of the document, we will entitle you to submit electronic filings to us and send you a confirming email.
ESCO or Utility required to file rates
Energy Service Companies (ESCOs) must register in order to enter rates for offers. To be authorized to enter ESCO rates, ESCO rate filers are asked to complete and submit a Contact Form to NYS DPS. The form and instructions can be found at our
Electronic Filing Registration Instructions
page. After receipt and review of the document, we will entitle you to electronically submit rates to us and send you a confirming email.
