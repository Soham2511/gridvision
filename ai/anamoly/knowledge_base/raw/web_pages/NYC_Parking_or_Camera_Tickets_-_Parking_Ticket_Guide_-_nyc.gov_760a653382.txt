Frequently asked questions
Expand all
How do I find my violation number?
You need your 10-digit violation number or license plate number to pay your fine.
Parking tickets
The number is beside the barcode on the side or bottom of the ticket. You do not need to type the hyphen.
Camera violations
The number is in the top right of the page titled “Notice of Liability.” It is labeled NOL # or Notice #.
What happens if I don’t pay?
If you don’t pay or dispute your fine within 30 days, you will be charged late fees. Refer to the tables below for penalty schedules. If your ticket is entered
into judgment

Why can’t I find my ticket information?
It can take up to three weeks for your ticket to appear in the online system.
You can pay your ticket before it appears by entering the violation number and fine amount from the paper ticket
into CityPay
. Please note that you can only pay in advance by searching the violation number, not your license plate number.
This information is only for NYC parking or camera tickets. If you received a moving violation ticket from a police officer, you must
pay through the DMV.
How does disputing work?
You request a hearing and submit evidence.
An administrative judge reviews your case and decides whether to dismiss your ticket.
If your ticket is dismissed, you do not have to pay.
If your ticket is not dismissed, you’ll have to pay the fine and any penalties charged before you requested a hearing.
I paid my ticket, why does it still say I owe money?
Payments can take up to two weeks to be processed and show up in the system. Check your bank or credit card account to confirm funds were withdrawn.
If you’ve paid in full and the ticket still appears after two weeks,
submit an online request for assistance
.
Are there fees for paying a ticket?
There is a 2% processing fee if you pay with a credit card, debit card, Venmo, or PayPal. There are no fees for paying by check, electronic check, or money order.
Watch out for private companies that charge extra fees for paying your ticket. Paying directly is the safest and most affordable option.
