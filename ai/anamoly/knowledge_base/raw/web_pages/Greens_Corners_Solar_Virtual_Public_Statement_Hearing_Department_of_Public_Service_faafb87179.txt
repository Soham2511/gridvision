Case Information
Case 23-E-0587
- Petition of Greens Corners Solar LLC for a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity and for an Order Granting Lightened Regulation Pursuant to Public Service Law Section 68.
Comments are requested concerning a petition filed by Greens Corners Solar, LLC (Greens Corners) seeking lightened regulation as wholesale generator, as well as a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity pursuant to Section 68 of the Public Service Law (PSL). Greens Corners seeks authorization to construct and operate an up to 140-megawatt solar photovoltaic energy generating project in the Towns of Hounsfield and Watertown, Jefferson County, New York (the Project). The Project is composed of approximately 509,650 bifacial solar panels affixed to a single axis tracking racking system, which will enable the panels to follow the sun’s path, on approximately 800 acres of land. It will connect to the existing transmission system through a new point of interconnection on National Grid’s Coffeen to West Admas 115- kilovolt electricity circuit.
Construction and operation of the Project would be subject to the terms and conditions of the Siting Permit that was issued by the Office of Renewable Energy Siting (ORES) on September 22, 2022, as modified by ORES on February 27, 2024. The scope of the Public Service Commission’s (Commission) review of the petition will not duplicate the issues that were previously considered and decided by ORES.
