How to Post Comments and Receive Updates on PSC Cases
Learn how to post comments and receive updates using the Department of Public Service Document and Matter Management (DMM) system.
Post Comments
Members of the public can post comments on each case before the Public Service Commission within the Document and Matter Management system (DMM).
Visit the
case search page
- enter the case number or a key word to find the case you would like to comment on.
Once on the case files page, click on the "Post Comments" button in the upper right hand corner (below the navigation).
You must fill in your name, address and email address to post your comment.
You have the option to add an attachment with your comment.
Receive Updates on Cases
Members of the public can be notified and receive emails about cases and matters before the Public Service Commission by signing up to receive updates. You can sign up with your email address, or by using Subscription management within DMM.
Using Email to Subscribe to the Service List
Complete the
Service List Request Form
as directed
Email form to
[email protected]
Please allow at least 2 – 3 business days for processing
Using Subscription Management in DMM
While on a case page, click on "Subscribe to Service List," in the upper right of the page, then in the next window scroll to the bottom and choose "Subscribe to Service List"
If you have a NY.Gov ID account
Enter your username and password and sign in
If you do not have a NY.Gov ID account, visit the
DMM Login help page
for instructions
Once your account is created, navigate to
https://documents.dps.ny.gov/custom/?APP=nyappdpsdmm
Enter your username and password and sign in
Choose "Manage Subscription" then "Add Service List Subscription"
Enter Matter or Case number and click on GO
Complete form information and choose "Subscribe"
Review Service List grid for service list subscription information
