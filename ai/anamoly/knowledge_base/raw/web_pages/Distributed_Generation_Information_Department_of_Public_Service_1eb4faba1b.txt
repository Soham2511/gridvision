NYS Standardized Interconnection Requirements (SIR)
In 1999, the Commission established Standardized Interconnection Requirements
and Application Process for New Distributed Generators and/or Energy Storage Systems 5 MW or Less Connected in Parallel with Utility Distribution Systems (SIR)
to allow such Distributed Energy Resources (DER) to interconnect to the electric system and help meet the State’s renewable energy goals.
DPS administers two stakeholder groups, the Interconnection Technical and Interconnection Policy working groups, that develop recommendations for improving the SIR interconnection process through industry and electric utility collaboration. Below you will find links to the working groups and other interconnection resources.
