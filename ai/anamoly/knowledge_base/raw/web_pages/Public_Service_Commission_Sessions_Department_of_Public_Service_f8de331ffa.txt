Public Service Commission Sessions
New York State Public Service Commission
Sessions and Materials
The New York State Public Service Commission sessions are held once a month both in person and virtually. You can watch the session webcast, which will be linked to within each calendar event. Webcasts with Closed Captioning will be available within 5-days after the event is over.
You can access PSC past sessions, agendas, transcripts, meeting videos and other materials. Each past PSC session page also includes the agenda, transcript and video for that session. Upcoming PSC Sessions are listed below.
